0 → Malignant
1 → Benign

🟢 Tumor -> Benign

Benign = not cancerous

A benign tumor is generally non-cancerous. It can grow, but it typically does not invade nearby tissues or spread to other parts of the body.


🔴Tumor -> Malignant

Malignant = cancerous

A malignant tumor is cancerous. Cancer cells can invade surrounding tissues and may spread to other parts of the body.
